# The Diagnostic fee is an honorarium, not a deposit

The Diagnostic is prepaid. Cancel at least 24 hours before the session and the fee is refunded. After that, and after a held session, the fee is kept — on fit, on no-fit, and if they never buy Track. The one-pager is the delivery.

Crediting it toward the first Track month turns the Diagnostic into a discount. Refunding a held session on no-fit turns it into a free trial with extra steps.

Rejected: credit to Track, refund after a held session, pay-if-you-liked-it.
