# Do not sell a Diagnostic into a closed cap

A Diagnostic is an entry to the Track. If no mentee slot is free or about to free, there is no entry — only a free waitlist.

Selling a 75-minute review while keeping the fee, with nowhere to place a mentee, is a different product. That product is out of scope.

Rejected: always-on Diagnostic, paid waitlist, “review even if the Track is full”.
