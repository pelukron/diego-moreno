# Waitlist is FIFO; a stalled head is dropped

The Diagnostic waitlist is free and FIFO. When a slot is free or about to free, only the head is offered a Diagnostic. If they do not schedule inside the 7-day offer window, they leave the queue and the next head is offered.

A slot is about to free only on a hard signal (cancel notice, stated non-renewal) or because Diego already decided to cut for no-fit or ghosting. A quiet month-end is not a signal.

Rejected: opaque “I’ll ping you”, a head that blocks the inventory forever, selling Diagnostics because a month *might* end.
